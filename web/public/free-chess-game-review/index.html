Free chess game review, as many games as you like

Import a game from Chess.com, Lichess or a PGN, and elo101 labels every move, shows both players' accuracy and the moment the game turned, then lets you retry your mistakes. No account, no daily limit.

Review a game nowFree · no account · runs in your browser

What you get

  • Every move labelled from Brilliant to Blunder, judged for players of your rating, with the better move shown on the board.
  • A plain explanation of what went wrong, such as "allows mate in 2", and what was best instead.
  • Accuracy and game rating for both players.
  • A chances graph showing who was likely to win after every move.
  • Retry mode: jump back to your mistakes and find the better move yourself.
  • Works on your phone and on any computer with a modern browser.

How to review a game

  1. Open elo101. Nothing to install or sign up for.
  2. Pick your game. Type your Chess.com or Lichess username and choose a recent game, paste a game link, or paste a PGN.
  3. Read the review. Step through the moves, open the summary, and press Retry on any mistake.

Why it's free and unlimited

The chess engine (Stockfish 18) runs inside your browser, using your own computer's processor. There's no server analysing your games, so there's nothing to ration. Your games are never uploaded for analysis.

Labels for your level, not a grandmaster's

Most reviews give one engine score whoever is playing. elo101 learned from millions of real Lichess games how often players at each rating actually win from a position, and judges moves by that. A move that throws away a win for a 1200 player is labelled like it. How it works →

Questions

Is elo101 really free and unlimited?

Yes. There's no account, no subscription and no daily limit. The analysis runs on your own device, so reviewing more games costs nothing.

Do I need an account?

No. Type a Chess.com or Lichess username to list recent public games, paste a game link, or paste a PGN.

How accurate is it?

On a fixed benchmark of 450 real games, every blunder elo101 flags is also a blunder on Lichess, and all 64 hand-checked test positions get the right label. Accuracy scores use Lichess's published formula and land within about 3 points of Lichess's.

Are my games uploaded?

No. Games are fetched from Chess.com or Lichess and analysed in your browser. The site uses anonymous usage analytics; see the privacy page.

Which chess engine does it use?

Stockfish 18, compiled to WebAssembly so it runs in the browser on every CPU core.
