Lichess game review, with labels for your rating

Lichess's computer analysis marks inaccuracies, mistakes and blunders. elo101 adds the rest: Brilliant, Great, Best and Miss, labels judged for your rating, a game rating for both players and a mode to retry your mistakes.

Review a game nowFree · no account · runs in your browser

Bring in a Lichess game

  1. By username. Choose the Lichess tab on elo101, type your username and pick a recent game.
  2. By link. Paste any lichess.org/… game link.
  3. With the Chrome extension. When your game ends on Lichess, click the elo101 button to open its review.

What elo101 adds to Lichess analysis

  • Ten move labels instead of three, including Brilliant, Great and Miss. What they mean →
  • Judged for your rating: the model learned from millions of Lichess games how often players at each level actually win from a position.
  • Chances, not just centipawns: a graph of who was likely to win, using both ratings and the clocks.
  • Game rating: roughly what rating each player played like in this game.
  • Retry mode to find the better move yourself.

Close to Lichess where it should be

Accuracy uses Lichess's published formula. On a benchmark of real games, elo101's accuracy is within about 3 points of Lichess's, and every blunder elo101 flags is also a blunder on Lichess. The engine runs in your browser, so a review doesn't depend on Lichess's server analysis being requested.

Questions

How is this different from Lichess's computer analysis?

Lichess shows inaccuracies, mistakes and blunders from one engine score. elo101 adds Brilliant, Great, Best, Excellent, Good, Book and Miss, judges moves for the players' ratings, and adds a game rating and a retry mode.

Does it use Lichess's own analysis?

No. elo101 runs its own Stockfish 18 in your browser on every move, so any game can be reviewed right away.

Is the accuracy the same as on Lichess?

It uses the same published formula on elo101's own engine evaluations, so it lands close: within about 3 points on average.

Do I need a Lichess account?

No. Public games can be found by username or link without logging in anywhere.
