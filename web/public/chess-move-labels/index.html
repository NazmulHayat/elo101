Chess move labels explained

Game reviews label every move, from Brilliant (!!) to Blunder (??). Here is what each label means, how it's decided, and why the same move can get a different label at a different rating.

Every label at a glance

Each move is compared with the engine's best move. Most labels depend on how much of your expected score the move gave away. Expected score is how many points you'd expect from the position: a win is 1, a draw ½ and a loss 0, so 0.70 means you'd score about 70%.

What makes a move Brilliant?

A Brilliant move is a real sacrifice that works. In elo101 it must be the engine's best move, give up at least two points of material (a minor piece for a pawn, or more), and the engine's main line must show the opponent taking the material while you stay down for at least three more moves, or deliver mate. A quick trick that wins the piece straight back is Best or Great, not Brilliant.

What makes a move Great?

Great marks the only good move: every alternative is much worse, or it's the one move that keeps you winning or out of a losing position. Forced moves, simple recaptures and mate in one don't count.

What does Miss mean?

Miss means your opponent just made a real mistake, and your reply gave most of that gift back. You didn't necessarily blunder: you simply didn't punish their error.

Mistake vs Blunder

Both lose something. A mistake gives away 10–20% of your expected score, like going from clearly better to roughly equal. A blunder gives away 20% or more, like hanging a piece or allowing mate.

Why the label depends on your rating

The same engine evaluation means different things at different levels. Being a piece up is almost always a win for two 2200 players, but much less certain for two 800 players. elo101 learned from millions of real games how often players at each rating win from each position, so a move is judged by what it costs players like you. Questions

What is a brilliant move in chess?

A brilliant move (!!) is a sound sacrifice: the best move in the position, which gives up material that the opponent takes, while the position still holds or wins.

What is a great move in chess?

A great move (!) is the only good move in the position, or the one move that keeps a winning position or avoids a losing one.

What does Miss mean in a chess game review?

Miss means your opponent made a mistake and your reply failed to take advantage of it, giving back most of what they gave away.

What is the difference between a mistake and a blunder?

A mistake gives away 10–20% of your expected score; a blunder gives away 20% or more, such as losing a piece or allowing mate.

What is a book move?

A book move is a known opening move, taken from an opening book of established theory.

What is an inaccuracy in chess?

An inaccuracy (?!) is a small error that gives away 5–10% of your expected score: not losing, but not the best.
