Three ways to bring in a Chess.com game

  1. By username. Choose the Chess.com tab on elo101, type your username and pick from your recent games.
  2. By link. Paste a chess.com/game/live/… link. elo101 asks for one of the players' usernames to find it in Chess.com's public archive.
  3. With the Chrome extension. When your game ends, an elo101 button appears on Chess.com. One click opens the review. It also appears next to Chess.com's "Daily limit reached" message.

Chess.com's public archive can take a few minutes to update after a game ends. Daily (correspondence) games aren't supported by link yet; paste the PGN instead.

The labels you know

elo101 uses the move labels Chess.com players are used to: Brilliant, Great, Best, Excellent, Good, Book, Inaccuracy, Mistake, Miss and Blunder. The rules behind them are elo101's own, published openly, and moves are judged for your rating. What every label means →

What's different from Chess.com Game Review

  • No daily limit and no membership.
  • Runs on your device: the analysis happens in your browser.
  • Open methods: the model, the label rules and the test results are all on GitHub.
  • Accuracy uses Lichess's published formula, because Chess.com's is private. Expect elo101's accuracy to differ from Chess.com's by several points on the same game.

Questions

Is this Chess.com's Game Review?

No. elo101 is an independent, free tool and isn't affiliated with Chess.com. It reads your public games from Chess.com's public archive and reviews them in your browser.

Is there a daily limit?

No. You can review as many Chess.com games as you like.

Will the labels match Chess.com's exactly?

Not always. elo101 uses the same label names and bands, but its own published rules and model, so a few moves can come out differently. Blunders and mistakes usually agree.

Why can't it find my game?

Chess.com's public archive can take a little while to update after a game ends. Try again in a few minutes, enter either player's username, or paste the PGN.

Can I review Chess.com daily games?

Not by link yet. Download or copy the game's PGN from Chess.com and paste it into elo101.
